## Asset Management Notes

- All hardware assets are tracked using the internal IT Asset Management System (ITAM).  
- Warranty renewals are checked quarterly and escalated 30 days before expiry.  
- Laptops are refreshed every 3–4 years depending on condition and model performance.  
- Retired assets are securely wiped and disposed of according to e-waste compliance policy.  
- Vendor relationships maintained for Dell, HP, Lenovo, Cisco, and Microsoft through annual procurement contracts.  
- Software license usage is audited quarterly to ensure compliance.
