# HR and IT Policies (Expanded - Generic Corporate IT)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This document defines the corporate Human Resources and Information Technology policies that govern employee behavior, technology usage, data handling, and workplace conduct.  
All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors must comply with these policies to ensure security, efficiency, and ethical operations within the organization.

---

## 2. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

### Purpose
To establish proper guidelines for the use of company systems, networks, and digital resources.

### Rules
- Company-provided equipment must be used only for authorized business purposes.  
- Users must not install or use unapproved software or browser extensions.  
- Accessing offensive, illegal, or inappropriate websites from company systems is prohibited.  
- Email systems must be used for professional communication only.  
- Sharing internal information or documents outside the company without authorization is not permitted.  
- Personal cloud storage (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox) must not be used for work-related data.  

### Responsibility
Employees are responsible for adhering to AUP at all times. Violations will result in disciplinary action, up to termination.

---

## 3. Remote Work and Hybrid Policy

### Purpose
To define security and performance expectations for employees working remotely or under hybrid arrangements.

### Rules
- Employees must connect to corporate systems via secure VPN.  
- Only company-approved devices with up-to-date antivirus and firewall protection are permitted.  
- Confidential data must not be downloaded, printed, or stored locally on personal systems.  
- Video conferencing tools (Teams, Zoom) should be used with discretion in shared environments.  
- Employees must remain reachable during assigned work hours and maintain communication discipline.  

### Responsibility
IT Security Team will monitor remote access logs. Employees are responsible for device compliance.

---

## 4. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy

### Purpose
To regulate the use of personal devices (phones, tablets, laptops) for accessing company data and applications.

### Rules
- Devices must be registered with the IT department before connecting to corporate resources.  
- Mobile Device Management (MDM) software will be installed for security enforcement.  
- The company reserves the right to wipe corporate data remotely in case of device loss or theft.  
- Jailbroken or rooted devices are strictly prohibited.  
- Personal devices must adhere to password and encryption standards.  

### Responsibility
Users are responsible for maintaining their personal devices and ensuring security compliance at all times.

---

## 5. Access Control and Privilege Management

### Purpose
To protect information systems through role-based access and strict privilege management.

### Rules
- Access to systems is granted based on job role and departmental requirements.  
- Administrative or elevated access requires formal approval from management.  
- Shared or generic accounts are not permitted under any circumstance.  
- Access is revoked immediately upon employee resignation, termination, or role change.  
- Access audits will be conducted quarterly by IT Security.  

### Responsibility
Managers are accountable for requesting and validating user access. IT controls the implementation and monitoring.

---

## 6. Data Protection and Privacy Policy

### Purpose
To ensure responsible handling of confidential, personal, and business-sensitive information.

### Rules
- Confidential data must be classified and labeled appropriately (e.g., Public, Internal, Confidential).  
- Sensitive data must only be stored on encrypted drives or company-approved cloud platforms.  
- Data sharing outside the company must be authorized and logged.  
- Printed documents containing confidential data must be shredded after use.  
- Screens should be locked when employees are away from their desks.  

### Responsibility
All employees share responsibility for protecting company data. Data breaches must be reported immediately to the IT Security Team.

---

## 7. Employee Onboarding and Offboarding

### Purpose
To ensure systematic provisioning and deactivation of user access during employment lifecycle changes.

### Onboarding Rules
- New employees will receive a standard IT asset kit including laptop, peripherals, and software licenses.  
- Access rights will be created based on department and position.  
- Orientation will include training on IT security and HR conduct.  

### Offboarding Rules
- Assets and ID cards must be returned on or before the last working day.  
- User accounts will be disabled within 2 hours of employee exit confirmation.  
- Email forwarding or data transfer will be performed under supervision.  

### Responsibility
HR coordinates with IT to ensure timely completion of all onboarding and offboarding processes.

---

## 8. Code of Conduct and Disciplinary Policy

### Purpose
To maintain professional integrity and ethical standards across all levels of the organization.

### Rules
- Employees must maintain professionalism and respect in all forms of communication.  
- Harassment, discrimination, or workplace misconduct will not be tolerated.  
- Use of company assets for personal gain or outside business is prohibited.  
- Repeated violation of IT or HR policies may result in suspension or termination.  
- False reporting or misuse of complaint channels is considered a violation.  

### Responsibility
HR manages conduct investigations; IT assists with system-level audit trails when misconduct involves digital evidence.

---

## 9. Policy Compliance and Review

### General Guidelines
- All policies are reviewed annually by HR and IT leadership.  
- Changes to these policies must be communicated company-wide via internal portal or email.  
- Employees must acknowledge receipt and understanding of updated policies.  
- Failure to comply with any section of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination.

---

## 10. Summary
These HR and IT policies serve as a framework for operational integrity, information protection, and professional conduct.  
Employees are expected to uphold these policies to ensure a secure, respectful, and efficient work environment.
